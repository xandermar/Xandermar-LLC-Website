## From Ticket to Finished Work

After setup, the loop is intentionally short:

1. **Create a ticket** in Jira with what you need built.
2. **Change the status to “In Progress”** to kick off automated development.
3. **Check your environment** to see the finished work—often in just minutes.

That’s it. NoDevTeam handles the handoff between planning, coding agents, and your repository so your team can focus on outcomes instead of orchestration.
